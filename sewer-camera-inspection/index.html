Sewer Camera Inspection in Zephyrhills, FL

A camera on a flexible cable shows what is wrong inside a drain or sewer line without digging. Use it after a backup, before buying a home or before agreeing to an expensive repair.

A sewer camera inspection answers the question that cleaning and guessing cannot: what is actually in the pipe. We feed a waterproof camera through the cleanout and watch the line on a monitor. A locator on the surface marks where the camera is, so a problem seen on screen can be found above ground.

Most inspections follow a backup, but it is also a smart step before buying a house or paying for a large repair. If sewage is backing up right now, call (813) 471-5608 and ask for the emergency crew. The line is open 24/7. You can read about repair options on our sewer line repair page.

What the camera can show

When an inspection is worth it

Good reasons to look inside the line

  • A main line backup or repeated drain clogs.
  • Buying an older home, where the line may be clay, cast iron or fiber.
  • Planning a large repair and wanting a second opinion.
  • Persistent sewer odor in the yard or house.
  • A soggy patch of lawn above the line.
  • Planning new landscaping or construction near the line.

Buying a home in Zephyrhills

A home inspection usually does not include the underground sewer line. Homes from earlier decades near downtown and older neighborhoods may have materials that crack and scale with age. A camera inspection gives you a video record of the line before closing. If the pipe has serious problems, you have information to negotiate with, or to budget for.

Newer homes have PVC lines, which hold up well. Even so, poor bedding or construction damage can leave a belly or an offset that a camera catches early.

What happens during the visit

  1. Locate the access point. We find the cleanout or pull a toilet if there is none.
  2. Run the camera. We push the camera along the line and watch the monitor.
  3. Mark problem spots. A locator tells us the depth and position of anything we see.
  4. Review together. We show you the video and explain what each finding means.
  5. Give options. You hear whether to monitor, clean or repair, with prices.

What affects the price

Cost factors on a camera inspection

  • The length of the line to inspect.
  • Access: a cleanout speeds the job.
  • Whether cleaning is needed first to give the camera a clear view.
  • Locating and marking problem spots on the surface.
  • After-hours dispatch for an emergency.

You get a clear price before work starts. The camera is only a diagnostic step, so we do not push repairs the video does not support. If the line is healthy, we tell you that, and you can stop there.

Owners of older homes, such as those in the Historic Downtown District, may use an inspection to learn what their drain line is made of before deciding on a repair.

Code and standards

A common way to describe pipe defects is the NASSCO Pipeline Assessment Certification Program (PACP) coding system, used for sewer inspection video. We describe what the camera shows in plain words, and we can discuss findings in those terms if your engineer, inspector or insurer asks.

FAQ

Questions about sewer camera inspection in Zephyrhills

Do you offer sewer camera inspections on short notice in Zephyrhills?

Often yes. Call (813) 471-5608. Emergencies are handled around the clock, and routine inspections are scheduled as available.

Will I get to see the video?

Yes. We review it with you, and you can ask what each finding means for repair or monitoring.

Does a camera inspection find every problem?

It finds what is visible inside the pipe along the path the camera travels. A line full of water or heavy buildup may need cleaning first.

Do I need an inspection when buying a house?

It is not required, but the sewer line is out of sight and expensive to repair. Many buyers choose to check it.

Can the camera find a leak?

It can show cracks and joint gaps that let sewage out. Water supply leaks are a different problem, covered by leak detection.
