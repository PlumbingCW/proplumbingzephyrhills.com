Most toilet problems fall into a few groups: a blockage, a running tank, a leak at the base and a weak flush. Some are quick fixes. Others signal an older toilet that has reached the end of its life. We tell you which you have before work begins.

If water is rising to the rim, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down, or turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise. Then call (813) 471-5608. The line is open 24/7. If other drains are backing up too, the clog is deeper, and drain cleaning is the right page.

Overflowing toilet: stop the water first

Steps for an overflow

  • Turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise until it stops.
  • If the valve is stuck, lift the tank lid and hold the flapper closed.
  • Do not flush again.
  • Put towels around the base and wear gloves, since the water may be contaminated.
  • Call us if you cannot clear it with a plunger after a few tries.

A toilet that keeps running

A running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons a week without making a sound. Add a few drops of food coloring to the tank, wait fifteen minutes without flushing, and look in the bowl. If color appears, the flapper is leaking. It is a cheap part, and replacing it often takes a single visit.

When to replace instead of repair

Replacement is worth considering when the porcelain is cracked, when parts for the model are hard to find or when the toilet needs repeated repair. Modern toilets use less water per flush, which can lower the water bill. We discuss what a new unit would cost against more repairs and let you choose. Our plumbing installation page covers new fixtures in general.

Height matters too. Comfort-height toilets sit a bit higher, which helps people with knee or hip trouble. Ask about it if you are replacing.

Hard clogs and what we do

A hand plunger and a toilet auger clear most single-toilet clogs. If the toilet clogs again, the trouble may be in the branch line or the main. In that case we use a longer cable and, if needed, a camera. Flushed wipes and products labeled flushable are a common cause of repeat clogs in Florida homes, and they do not break down the way toilet paper does.

Homes on septic systems

Many Zephyrhills properties outside the city sewer area use a septic system. Septic tanks do not like excess wipes, grease or chemicals. If your toilet backs up and other fixtures are slow, mention the septic system, since the tank may need service.

Code and standards

Federal rules limit new toilets to 1.6 gallons per flush, and the EPA's WaterSense label marks models that use 1.28 gallons per flush or less. That matters when you choose a replacement, since a newer toilet can lower the water bill, and the IPC sets the clearances around a toilet when it is installed.

FAQ

Questions about toilet repair in Zephyrhills

Do you handle toilet repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Zephyrhills?

Yes. Call (813) 471-5608 at any hour. Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet first.

Why does my toilet keep running?

Usually a worn flapper or fill valve. Both are inexpensive parts, and we can replace them in one visit.

Is a rocking toilet a real problem?

Yes. It can mean a failed wax ring or a loose flange, and water may be leaking under the floor. Do not wait.

Should I fix or replace an old toilet?

If it is cracked or needs repeated repairs, replacement is often the better value. We explain both options.

Can flushable wipes clog a toilet?

They can. They do not break down like toilet paper and are a frequent cause of repeat clogs.
