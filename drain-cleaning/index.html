Backup right now: what to do first

Before the plumber arrives

  • Stop running water, flushing and using the dishwasher or washer.
  • Keep children and pets out of any sewage that reaches the floor.
  • Skip chemical drain openers. They sit in the pipe and can burn the technician and the pipe.
  • If the toilet is close to overflowing, lift the tank lid and close the flapper, then shut the supply valve at the wall.
  • Photograph any water damage for your insurance claim.

One slow drain or the whole house?

The pattern tells us where the clog sits. One fixture that drains slowly has a branch line problem. Two or more fixtures that act up at once, a toilet that bubbles when the shower drains, or a floor drain that backs up when the washer runs means the blockage is lower down, at or past the point where the branches join.

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen lines clog because fats cool and coat the pipe wall. Each wash adds a layer until the opening shrinks to a few fingers wide. Running hot water does not fix it. Garbage disposals add ground food to the same line, so a disposal that hums but drains slowly is often a drain problem, not a motor problem.

If the disposal is the likely culprit, see our garbage disposal repair page.

How we clear a line

  1. Locate the cleanout. We find the closest cleanout or the trap and decide which cable and head to use.
  2. Run the cable. A motorized cable breaks through the blockage and measures how far down it sits.
  3. Inspect. On a repeat clog we feed a camera to see roots, a belly, a broken joint or buildup.
  4. Test the flow. We run water through the fixtures and confirm they drain before we leave.

What older Zephyrhills homes add to the problem

The city incorporated in 1914, and homes near the Historic District downtown predate modern PVC drain pipe. Older lines can include cast iron or clay in places, and those materials roughen and crack with age. Tree roots follow moisture into any joint that has opened. If your house is old and the drain keeps returning, a camera check tells you more than another round of snaking.

The homes around Gall Boulevard and the US 301 corridor span many decades, so the pipe material varies from block to block. A good plumber checks what is in the wall rather than assuming.

Code and standards

The IPC, which the Florida Building Code, Plumbing is based on, requires a trap on each fixture and sets where cleanouts must be provided on drain lines. A missing or buried cleanout is a common reason a drain job takes longer than it should, and adding one is worth discussing if your line has none.

Questions about drain cleaning in Zephyrhills

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Zephyrhills?

Yes. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and sewage backups get priority. Call (813) 471-5608 and tell us which fixtures are backing up.

Why does my drain keep clogging?

Repeat clogs usually point to buildup on the pipe wall, roots, or a damaged section. A camera inspection shows which. Our sewer line repair page covers what happens when a line needs more than cleaning.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaner first?

We advise against it. Chemicals can sit in a standing clog, damage older pipes and splash on the technician who opens the line.

What is the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?

A snake punches a hole through the clog. A jetter scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water and removes grease and scale. Read about hydro jetting for details.

Can you clear a drain in a manufactured home or older house?

Yes. Tell us the home type so we bring the right cable and know to expect older pipe material.
