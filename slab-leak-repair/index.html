Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Zephyrhills, FL

Most Florida homes sit on a concrete slab, and some of the plumbing runs through or beneath it. When a line under the floor leaks, the signs are a warm spot, a high bill or the sound of water. We locate and repair slab leaks in Zephyrhills.

A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs under or inside a home's concrete foundation. Slab-on-grade is the common foundation in Florida, so this is a local problem. Zephyrhills homes built on slabs can have supply lines in the concrete or in the soil just under it, and a pinhole there can run for weeks before anyone sees water.

If you hear water running with every tap closed, feel a warm patch on the floor, or see the floor covering lift, call (813) 471-5608. The line is open 24/7. First shut off the water at the main valve to stop the loss. For a broader check before you suspect the slab, see leak detection.

Slab leak warning signs

What homeowners notice

  • A warm or hot spot on the floor, often from a hot water line.
  • The sound of running water when every fixture is off.
  • A sudden jump in the water bill or a meter that keeps moving.
  • Damp carpet, a wet baseboard or tile that has cracked or lifted.
  • Low water pressure throughout the house.
  • A damp smell or mildew near an exterior wall.

Emergency steps while you wait

What to do for a suspected slab leak

  • Close the main water shutoff and open one faucet to drain the pressure.
  • Turn off the water heater if the hot side is the leak, because a heater cannot refill safely.
  • Move furniture and rugs off the wet area and lift what you can.
  • Photograph the damage and note the time you first noticed it.
  • Keep power off to any outlet near standing water.

Why slab lines fail

Slabs shift a little as soil moves. The ground around Zephyrhills is part of the limestone region of west-central Florida, and Pasco County has had sinkhole activity. In late summer 2019, 76 ground depressions opened in a Hudson neighborhood in Pasco County, and state geologists tied them to cave systems roughly 15 to 150 feet underground.

That is a dramatic example, and most slab leaks come from a quieter cause. Copper pipe corrodes. Older polybutylene lines in homes from about 1978 to 1995 can fail. A pipe rubbing against concrete wears through.

Repair options and honest trade-offs

A spot repair breaks a small opening in the slab, repairs the pipe and patches the concrete. It works for one isolated leak in a pipe that is otherwise healthy. A reroute abandons the leaking line and runs new pipe through the attic or walls, which avoids the slab.

A repipe replaces the system and is worth discussing when the pipe has failed more than once. See our repiping page for that path.

We will not push the biggest job. If the pipe is in good shape except for one spot, a spot repair is the sensible answer. If you have had two or three leaks in the same house, repeating spot repairs costs more over time than a reroute.

Houses where we see slab leaks around Zephyrhills

Homes from the decades before PVC and PEX, including many near downtown and in older subdivisions, can still have copper that is thirty or forty years old. Manufactured homes are built differently and usually have lines in the belly under the home instead of the slab, so a leak there is a different job. Tell us the home type on the call.

What affects the price

Cost factors on a slab leak job

  • The leak's location and depth, and the floor covering above it.
  • Whether a spot repair, reroute or repipe is chosen.
  • Flooring, cabinet and baseboard work after the repair.
  • Permit requirements, which vary by address.
  • After-hours dispatch for an emergency.

You get a clear price before work starts. Sudden water damage is often covered by homeowners insurance, but the repair itself may not be, so ask your carrier and keep your photos. If it is a larger loss, call the insurance company before you demolish anything.

Code and standards

The IPC sets requirements for water pipe materials and for pressure. Where static water pressure exceeds 80 psi, it calls for a pressure-reducing valve. High pressure is a known cause of leaks, so we check it after a slab repair, since fixing one pipe without checking the pressure can leave the next failure waiting.

FAQ

Questions about slab leak repair in Zephyrhills

Do you handle slab leak repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Zephyrhills?

Yes. Call (813) 471-5608 any time. Shut off the main valve first, then tell dispatch what you found.

How do you find a leak under concrete?

We isolate the line, listen with acoustic equipment and scan with a thermal camera. That narrows the leak to a small area before any concrete is opened.

Is a slab leak covered by insurance?

Often the resulting damage is covered and the pipe repair is not. Your policy decides, so call your carrier.

How long does a slab leak repair take?

A spot repair can take a day. A reroute or repipe takes longer. You will get a timeline with the quote.

Can a manufactured home have a slab leak?

Most manufactured homes run lines under the floor in the belly rather than in the slab. The signs are similar, and we adjust the approach.

Do you repair slab leaks in Wesley Chapel?

Yes. Many Wesley Chapel homes are on slabs. Call to schedule.
