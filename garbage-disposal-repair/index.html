Garbage disposals fail in a few predictable ways. The blades jam on something hard. The motor hums and will not turn. A seal gives out and water drips into the cabinet. Or the drain behind the disposal clogs and the sink fills with gray water. Each has a different fix.

If the sink is full of water or the disposal is leaking, switch off the disposal and its wall switch, then call (813) 471-5608. The line is open 24/7. Never put your hand in the disposal, even when it is off. If the problem is a clog past the unit, see drain cleaning.

Jam or leak tonight: safe steps

Before we arrive

  • Turn off the disposal switch and unplug it, or switch off its breaker.
  • Never reach into the disposal, even with the power off.
  • If it leaks, place a bucket under the unit and close the sink's water if you can.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into the sink.
  • Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit if the motor is silent.

What should and should not go down the disposal

Keep these out of the unit

  • Grease, fat and oil, which coat the pipe and clog the line.
  • Fibrous foods such as celery, corn husks and onion skins in large amounts.
  • Pasta, rice and coffee grounds, which swell and pack the trap.
  • Bones, fruit pits and hard shells.
  • Non-food items such as bottle caps and utensils.

Running cold water during use and for a few seconds after helps push waste down the line. A disposal is not a trash can, and the sink drain beyond it is only as large as a standard kitchen line. Grease is the worst offender, and it is a common cause of repeat kitchen clogs. If your kitchen line keeps backing up, hydro jetting may be worth discussing.

Repair or replace

A jammed disposal is often fixable in minutes and costs little. A leaking housing cannot be repaired. If the unit is ten or more years old, has failed twice or leaks from the bottom, replacement is usually the better value.

We explain what we found and what a new unit would involve. Horsepower, noise level and the feed type are the main choices, and we help you pick one that fits your household.

Installing a new disposal

Installation involves the mounting ring, the drain connection, an electrical connection and, in some kitchens, a dishwasher drain connection that has to be opened. If your kitchen has no disposal wiring, the electrical work may need a licensed electrician. We tell you what the job requires before we begin. For other fixture work, see plumbing installation.

Code and standards

The IPC covers how a dishwasher drain connects to a disposal and requires protection against backflow, using an air gap or a high loop where the code allows it. Wiring a disposal follows the NEC, which is why a missing outlet may call for an electrician.

FAQ

Questions about garbage disposal repair in Zephyrhills

Do you handle garbage disposal repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Zephyrhills?

Yes. Call (813) 471-5608 at any hour. Turn the unit off first.

Why does my disposal hum but not spin?

The blades are probably jammed. We free the jam from underneath and reset the unit. If the motor is burned out, it needs replacing.

Is it safe to reset the disposal myself?

Pressing the reset button with the power off is a common first step. Never put a hand into the unit.

Why is my sink backing up around the disposal?

The drain line past the unit is likely clogged. Grease and starchy food are common causes.

Can you install a disposal where there is none?

Often yes. It may need an electrical connection, and we tell you what is involved.
