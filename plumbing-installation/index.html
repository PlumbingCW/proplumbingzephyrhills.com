Installation covers the work of putting new plumbing in or replacing old fixtures. It sounds simple, and often it is. It also goes wrong when the supply lines are old, the shutoff valve is seized or the drain does not line up. A good installer checks all of this before connecting the new fixture.

Before the plumber arrives

How to prepare for an installation

  • Buy the fixture ahead if you have a specific model, and keep the box and manual.
  • Clear the area under the sink or around the toilet.
  • Locate the shutoff valve for the fixture and the main shutoff for the house.
  • Tell us if you have a septic system or a private well.
  • Ask about permit needs for larger work, like a new bathroom.

Supply lines and shutoff valves

Old shutoff valves and supply lines are the most common failure points in a plain swap. A valve that has not been turned in years can break or leak when you try to close it. We replace both when we install a new fixture, since the cost of a new line is small compared to a flood.

We use stainless braided lines for most fixtures, since they resist kinks and burst less often than thin plastic ones.

Remodels and new lines

Adding a bathroom, moving a sink or relocating a washer needs new supply and drain lines. In a slab home, new drains may require cutting the slab, and that changes the budget. We tell you early whether a layout change is simple or costly. Larger work can require a permit, so check with the City of Zephyrhills or Pasco County for your address before you start.

Water heaters and appliances

Water heaters are their own category. See water heater installation for the details. For water-using appliances, we connect dishwashers, washing machine boxes and ice makers, and we replace braided hoses that have aged out. A washing machine hose that bursts floods a laundry room quickly, so replacing it every few years is smart.

What affects the price

Cost factors on an installation

  • The fixture, whether you supply it or we do.
  • New shutoff valves, supply lines and drain fittings.
  • Access, such as a slab, a tight cabinet or a finished wall.
  • New lines or layout changes.
  • Permit needs and after-hours dispatch.

You get a clear price before work starts. We test every connection for leaks before we leave.

Code and standards

The IPC sets the rules for traps, vents, fixture clearances and shutoff valves. Fixtures and drinking water parts should carry the certifications the code references, such as NSF/ANSI 61 for components in contact with drinking water. Ask what applies to the fixture you are installing.

FAQ

Questions about plumbing installation in Zephyrhills

Can you install fixtures I bought myself?

Yes. Tell us the model and we will check that it fits your connections. We cannot warranty a fixture we did not supply, but our connections are tested.

Do you offer plumbing installation on short notice in Zephyrhills?

Often yes. Call (813) 471-5608. Emergencies are dispatched around the clock.

Do I need a permit to replace a toilet or faucet?

Usually not for a like-for-like swap. New lines, relocations and larger work may need one, so ask the building department for your address.

Should I replace the shutoff valve with a new fixture?

Yes, if it is old or does not turn smoothly. The part is inexpensive and prevents problems.

Can you connect a dishwasher or ice maker?

Yes. We connect the water and drain, and we replace worn supply hoses.
