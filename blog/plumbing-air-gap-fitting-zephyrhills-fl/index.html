Plumbing Air Gap Fitting in Zephyrhills, FL: What It Does and Where It Goes

An air gap is a small fitting on the sink, and its job is to keep drain water from ever flowing back into your dishwasher. It also solves a few common kitchen leaks.

Call (813) 471-5608

By the Zephyrhills Pro Plumbing team. Published November 13, 2026. Updated November 13, 2026.

An air gap is a short fitting, usually a chrome cylinder next to the kitchen faucet, that sits between the dishwasher and the sink drain or the garbage disposal. The dishwasher hose runs up into it, and a second hose runs from it down to the disposal or the drain.

Between the two hose connections is open air, which is the gap. If the sink drain backs up, the dirty water cannot travel backward into the dishwasher.

If your dishwasher drains poorly or water spills out of the air gap, a plumber can usually find the cause quickly. Our plumbing installation crews install and replace air gaps, and you can reach us 24/7 at (813) 471-5608 if a backup is flooding the kitchen.

Why a dishwasher needs protection

A dishwasher drains used water out through a hose. If the sink drain clogs, the dirty water can rise and flow backward through that hose into the dishwasher, bringing food waste with it. An air gap or a properly built high loop stops that. Both are plumbing code devices, and which one is accepted depends on the local code and the inspector.

How an air gap fails

Common air gap problems

  • Water spills from the cap when the dishwasher drains, which usually means a clog downstream.
  • A gurgling sound as water passes through.
  • A clogged internal tube from food residue.
  • Loose or cracked hoses under the sink.
  • A leak at the countertop hole where the fitting mounts.

An air gap that spills is not the cause of the problem. It is the warning. The blockage is usually in the garbage disposal inlet, the sink trap or the drain line beyond. Clearing it solves both. If the disposal itself is leaking or jammed, see our garbage disposal repair page.

Cleaning an air gap

  1. Pull the cap. Lift off the chrome cap and the inner cover. Some twist, and some pull straight up.
  2. Look inside. Look for food particles and slime.
  3. Clean the tube. Use a small brush or a bent pipe cleaner to clear the inlet and outlet.
  4. Check the hoses. Make sure the hoses are clamped and not kinked or sagging.
  5. Run the dishwasher. Run a cycle and watch for spills at the air gap.

Installing or replacing one

A new air gap needs a hole in the countertop or sink deck, often an existing faucet hole or sprayer hole. In stone countertops, drilling is not a casual job. The fitting then connects with two hoses and clamps. If your counter has no hole, a plumber may use a high loop instead, or the owner may drill the hole with the right tool.

Replacing a leaking air gap is more straightforward. The old one unscrews from below, and the new one mounts in the same hole. Replace the hoses at the same time if they are brittle or stained.

A mistaken fix to avoid

Some people plug an air gap with a cap or silicone because it spills. That removes the protection and leaves the underlying clog in place. Fix the blockage, not the symptom. If the air gap still spills after a clean sink drain, a plumber can check the disposal and the drain pipe for a restriction.

Kitchen remodel checklist for dishwasher drains

Questions for the installer

  • Will the dishwasher drain use an air gap or a high loop?
  • Is the disposal knockout plug removed if the dishwasher connects to it?
  • Are the hoses clamped and routed without sags or kinks?
  • Will the countertop be drilled, and who does it?
  • Is the drain trap and pipe sized for the sink and dishwasher together?

A remodel is the cheapest time to get this right, because the cabinet is empty and the counter may be open. Ask for the choice in writing, and take a photo of the finished drain connections for your records.

Code and standards

The IPC covers indirect waste connections such as a dishwasher drain and the backflow protection they need. The same code defines an air gap, and ASSE 1021 is the performance standard for drainage air gaps.

A local note on code and inspection

Florida's plumbing code is part of the Florida Building Code, and it covers how dishwasher drains must be protected. The wording and the inspector's reading can vary, so ask the City of Zephyrhills building department, or Pasco County for an address outside city limits, whether an air gap or a high loop is expected for your project.

Older homes may have a dishwasher draining directly into the disposal with a loop only. If you are remodeling the kitchen, it is a good time to ask about adding an air gap.

FAQ

Air gap questions from Zephyrhills homeowners

Is an air gap required in Zephyrhills?

Requirements depend on the code and the local inspector. Ask the building department about your project.

Why does water come out of my air gap?

A clog beyond the air gap is the usual cause, often in the disposal or the sink drain. Clear the clog and clean the air gap.

Can I replace an air gap myself?

Often yes. It is a two-hose connection and a threaded mount. A plumber can help if the countertop is hard to work with.

What if I have no air gap?

Many homes use a high loop. If the loop is loose or low, the dishwasher can get backflow from the sink.
