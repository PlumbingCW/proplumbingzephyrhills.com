Plumbing Repair for Frozen Pipes: A Zephyrhills, FL Homeowner Guide

Freezes are rare here, and that is the problem. Few Zephyrhills homes are built or insulated for one, so a single cold night can split a pipe.

Call (813) 471-5608

By the Zephyrhills Pro Plumbing team. Published November 13, 2026. Updated November 13, 2026.

A pipe freezes when the water inside turns to ice and expands. Ice blocks the flow, and the pressure it builds can split the pipe. The split often does not leak until the ice melts, which is when people discover the damage.

In Florida, where freezing nights come only now and then, the pipes at highest risk are the ones nobody thinks about: hose bibs, irrigation backflow assemblies, and lines in attics, garages and crawlspaces.

Signs a pipe is frozen

What to check on a cold morning

  • A faucet that gives a trickle or nothing, while others work normally.
  • Frost on a visible pipe, or a pipe that is cold and slightly bulging.
  • Odd noises or banging when you open the tap.
  • A smell from a drain, which can mean a frozen drain line.
  • Water stains appearing on a ceiling or wall after the thaw.

How to thaw it safely

  1. Open the faucet. Leave the tap open so melting water and steam have a place to go.
  2. Find the frozen section. Look for the cold, frosty part of the pipe, usually near an outside wall or in an unheated space.
  3. Warm it slowly. Use a hair dryer, a heating pad or warm towels. Start near the faucet and work toward the frozen part.
  4. Never use a flame. A torch can ignite framing or insulation and can damage the pipe.
  5. Watch for leaks. As the ice melts, check the pipe and the surrounding wall for water.

If you cannot find the frozen section, if the pipe is behind a wall or if you see a split, stop and call. Thawing a split pipe can flood the area, so shut the water at the main first.

After the thaw: look for damage

A pipe that has frozen once can have a hairline crack that only leaks under pressure. After thawing, turn the water back on slowly and look along the whole run. Check the ceiling below and the wall around it for drips. Watch the water meter with the house shut off for a few minutes. If it moves, water is escaping somewhere.

A split pipe is repaired by cutting out the damaged section and replacing it with new pipe and fittings. Copper, PEX and CPVC are all common in Florida homes, and a plumber matches what is already there. If the split is in a slab or a wall, our leak detection page covers how we find it.

Prepare before a freeze warning

When a freeze is forecast, disconnect and drain hoses, cover outdoor faucets and the irrigation backflow assembly, and open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls so room air can reach the pipes. Let a faucet drip slowly on the exposed line, since moving water is harder to freeze. If you leave town for the winter, ask a neighbor to check the house.

Do not turn off the water heater, but make sure you know where the main shutoff is. If a pipe bursts while you are away, that valve is your best tool.

When to call a plumber

Call when a pipe will not thaw, when you see a split, when water is leaking, when you hear water running in a wall or when the ceiling is stained. If you suspect a hidden leak after a freeze, a plumber can test the lines under pressure and find the problem before the damage spreads.

What a frozen irrigation system looks like

Irrigation backflow assemblies sit above ground, and their brass body can crack in a hard freeze. After a freeze, look for water spraying from the assembly or a wet area near it when the zone valves are off. If the assembly is cracked, turn off the irrigation supply and have it replaced. Some jurisdictions require a certified tester for a new assembly, so ask your water provider.

Pool equipment follows its own rules. Follow the pool maker's freeze guidance and run the pump if it recommends doing so. If you find a cracked pipe at the pool pad, close the supply valve first and then call a pool or plumbing professional.

Code and standards

The IPC says water pipes must not be installed in exterior walls, attics, crawlspaces or other places subject to freezing unless adequate provision is made to protect them. That is the code basis for insulating exposed pipe, and it is useful to know if you are remodeling.

A local note on Pasco County freezes

Freeze watches and warnings reached Pasco County in January 2026. A freeze in this part of Florida is short and may last only a few hours before sunrise, but a few hours is enough to freeze an exposed hose bib or an irrigation assembly. Check your outdoor fixtures when the forecast drops near freezing.

Many Zephyrhills homes, including manufactured homes with lines under the floor, have supply pipe in places that are not insulated for cold. Skirting and belly insulation help. Ask a plumber what your home needs.

FAQ

Frozen pipe questions from Zephyrhills homeowners

Will a frozen pipe always burst?

No. Pipes can freeze without bursting, but ice can crack them. You will not know until the water thaws and you test the line.

Can I use a heat gun to thaw a pipe?

Use a hair dryer on a low setting, not a heat gun or a torch. High heat can damage the pipe and start a fire.

Does homeowners insurance cover a burst pipe from a freeze?

Often it covers resulting water damage and not the pipe. Check your policy and call your carrier.

Should I leave faucets dripping during a freeze?

On exposed lines, a slow drip helps. Do not leave it dripping for days without a reason.
