About Zephyrhills Pro Plumbing

A local plumbing service with one number, a 24/7 dispatch line and a plain way of working.

Call (813) 471-5608

Zephyrhills Pro Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 6305 Gall Boulevard in Zephyrhills. We answer calls on one number, (813) 471-5608, around the clock, and we send plumbers to homes and small businesses in Zephyrhills and nearby towns in eastern Pasco County.

This page tells you how the business works so you know what to expect before you call. We do not list awards, years in business or review counts here, because we would rather say less than say something you cannot check.

How a call works

  1. You call. A dispatcher answers, asks what is happening and tells you what to shut off if needed.
  2. A plumber is assigned. You get the earliest available slot. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.
  3. The plumber checks the problem. You hear what is wrong and what it will cost before work starts.
  4. The work is done and tested. We test the repair before we leave and explain what we found.

What we handle

We repair and replace water heaters, clear drains and sewer lines, find and fix leaks, replace failed pipes and fixtures, and install new fixtures and water treatment equipment. We also take after-hours calls from small commercial properties. The full list is on our plumbing services page.

What we do not do

Work we send elsewhere

  • Gas line leaks. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
  • Septic tank pumping and drain field repair, which are separate trades.
  • Fire sprinkler systems and large new-construction plumbing.
  • Electrical work beyond a simple connection for a plumbing appliance.
  • Pool structure or plaster leaks.

How we handle pricing and standards

You get a clear price before work starts. After-hours dispatch can change the price, and we say so up front. Plumbing work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, and local permit rules where they apply. Permits depend on your address, so ask the City of Zephyrhills building department or Pasco County for addresses outside city limits.

Local ground we work on

Zephyrhills includes the Historic District downtown, manufactured-home communities, newer subdivisions and commercial strips along Gall Boulevard and US 301. Each needs a different eye. Older pipe corrodes. Manufactured homes carry lines in the belly. Newer homes fail at fittings and water heaters.

We tailor the repair to what is in the wall. That is why dispatch asks about the age and type of the home before the plumber is sent, and why the answer changes what goes on the truck.

Who calls us

Most calls come from homeowners, but the mix is wider than that. Seasonal residents call when they return to a house that sat closed for months. Landlords and property managers call about a tenant's backup. Small businesses along Gall Boulevard and US 301 call about restrooms, water heaters and floor drains. Each caller needs a slightly different conversation, so dispatch asks the property type first.

Standards and honest limits

We follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, and the manufacturer's instructions for the equipment we install. We do not quote local water hardness numbers because we could not source one for every address. If you want to know what is in your water, test your tap or ask your water utility for its latest quality report. That is the same advice we give on our water softener and filtration page.

We also do not promise arrival times. Traffic, weather and earlier jobs move the schedule, and a number we cannot keep helps nobody. Dispatch will give you the earliest window it can and will call if it changes.

Hours and how to reach us

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays. Email at contact@proplumbingzephyrhills.com is checked during the day and is for questions that are not urgent. The office address is 6305 Gall Boulevard, Zephyrhills, FL 33542. For details on getting here, see our contact page.

What you can expect after the work

At the end of a visit

  • A plain explanation of what was wrong and what was done.
  • A test of the repair before the plumber leaves.
  • Notes on anything else that needs attention, with no pressure to buy it.
  • A written record of the work if you need one for insurance or a landlord.
  • A number to call if the problem returns.
