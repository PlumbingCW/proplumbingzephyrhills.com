Sewer line cleaning clears the main drain that carries waste from your house out to the sewer connection. It is a bigger machine and a longer reach than a sink or tub snake. When a main line clogs, you do not get a slow drain in one bathroom. You get backups at the lowest fixture in the house and gurgling in the rest.

Call (813) 471-5608 any time. We dispatch emergency crews around the clock. If the line keeps clogging after cleaning, see sewer line repair, since the pipe may be damaged.

Common causes of a clogged main

What blocks a main sewer line

  • Tree and shrub roots entering through joints.
  • Grease and wipes that collect in a low spot.
  • A belly in the line where solids settle.
  • Toys, sanitary products and other items that were flushed.
  • Scale buildup in older cast iron.

A single clog from a flushed item can clear in one visit. Roots, grease and bellies return unless the cause is dealt with. That is why we look at the pipe after cleaning.

While you wait for the crew

Before the plumber arrives

  • Stop running water in the house.
  • Find the cleanout, a capped pipe usually near the foundation or at the property edge.
  • Clear the area around the cleanout of toys, furniture or stored items.
  • Keep people and pets away from any overflow.
  • Do not pour drain chemicals into the line.

How we clear the main line

  1. Open the cleanout. We remove the cap and release pressure safely.
  2. Cut through the blockage. A cable machine with a cutter head clears roots and solids.
  3. Flush and restore flow. We run water and confirm the line drains freely.
  4. Inspect with a camera. The video shows whether the pipe is sound and why it clogged.
  5. Recommend next steps. Jetting, repair or nothing at all, depending on what we see.

If the house has a septic system

Some Zephyrhills properties, especially on larger lots outside the city sewer area, use a septic tank rather than a municipal sewer. A septic system has a tank and a drain field, and a backup there may mean a full tank or a failing field instead of a clogged line.

We clear the line between the house and the tank. Pumping the tank is a separate service, and a failing drain field is a different repair. Tell us on the call which type you have.

Preventing the next clog

A few habits keep a main line open. Flush only toilet paper. Put kitchen grease in the trash, not the drain. Keep trees and large shrubs away from the line when you plant. If roots are an ongoing problem, ask about a scheduled cleaning or a camera check each year or two.

What affects the price

Cost factors on a sewer cleaning

  • Whether there is a usable cleanout.
  • The length of line and the nature of the clog.
  • The machine needed, from cable to jetter.
  • A camera inspection after the clearing.

You get a clear price before work starts. For commercial properties, see commercial plumbing.

Code and standards

The IPC requires cleanouts at set points on a building drain, and a well-placed one is the difference between a quick clearing and a long one. If the cleanout on your property is buried or capped with no access, ask about exposing it.

FAQ

Questions about sewer line cleaning in Zephyrhills

Do you handle sewer line cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Zephyrhills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. Call (813) 471-5608.

Where is my sewer cleanout?

Usually a capped pipe outside near the house or along the line to the street. If there is none, we can access the line through a toilet. Installing a cleanout is worth discussing.

Will roots come back after you cut them?

They can. Roots return if the pipe has an opening. A camera check shows how bad the entry is and whether lining or repair is worth considering.

How is sewer cleaning different from drain cleaning?

Sewer cleaning reaches the main line with heavier machines. Drain cleaning handles individual fixtures and branch lines.

My house has a septic tank. Can you still help?

We clear the line to the tank. Tank pumping and drain field work are separate services, and we tell you when that is the likely cause.
