Where Silver Oaks is

Silver Oaks is a residential community in Zephyrhills with the ZIP code 33542. Listing addresses place homes along Silver Oaks Drive and Northlake Drive. We could not find a mapped boundary to cite, so give your street address when you call and do not rely on the community name alone.

City of Zephyrhills annexation records show the community grew in pieces. Ordinance 846-03 annexed a parcel of about 2.67 acres for The Links of Silver Oaks. Ordinance 896-04, recorded in October 2004, annexed about 9.9 acres for Cottages at Silver Oaks Phase II.

Several sections, several build dates

Listing data names sections such as Silver Oaks Village, Brentwood at Silver Oaks, Laurelwood at Silver Oaks and Stephens Glen at Silver Oaks, alongside the Cottages. Listings show both single-family homes and townhomes in these sections. Because they were added over time, the age of your own house is a better guide than the community name.

You can look up the year your home was built on the Pasco County Property Appraiser site. Knowing it helps you ask better questions about the water heater, the fixtures and the drain lines.

Questions worth asking first

  • Is the problem inside your home, or in a line that serves more than one home.
  • What do your HOA documents say about who repairs what.
  • How old is the water heater, and where is its shutoff.
  • Does the problem happen at one fixture, or throughout the house.

Plumbing services that fit this kind of community

Services that suit this setting include drain cleaning for slow or backing-up lines, water heater replacement when a tank fails, toilet repair for running or leaking toilets and leak detection for water you cannot see.

In attached homes, a drain backup may involve a line shared with a neighbor. If more than one home is affected, tell the HOA or the property manager as well as the plumber, because a shared line can change who is responsible for the repair.

Backup in more than one drain?

Stop running water, avoid flushing and call. A backup in several drains at once can point to the main line and not to a single fixture.

FAQ

Silver Oaks plumbing questions

Is Silver Oaks inside the City of Zephyrhills?

City ordinances annexed parcels for The Links of Silver Oaks and Cottages at Silver Oaks Phase II, and listings give Zephyrhills addresses with ZIP 33542. The full boundary is not mapped here, so confirm your address with the city if it matters for a permit or a utility question.

Are all Silver Oaks homes the same age?

Probably not. The annexation records show sections added at different times. Check the year built for your address on the Pasco County Property Appraiser site instead of assuming.

Who fixes a leak in a shared wall or line?

It depends on your HOA documents and where the line serves more than one home. Tell the HOA or property manager early, and describe the problem to the plumber so the repair can be scoped correctly.

Can I call about a problem in Silver Oaks?

Yes. Give your street address and describe the problem, and you will hear what to expect.
