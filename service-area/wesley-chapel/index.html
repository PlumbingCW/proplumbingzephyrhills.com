Why newer homes still need emergency plumbers

A house built in the last twenty years rarely has corroded galvanized pipe. Its trouble spots are different. Builder-grade water heaters often start failing after ten to fifteen years. Plastic supply lines, braided hoses and shutoff valves can crack or loosen. Water pressure that runs high wears fittings faster than most owners expect.

Homes of this era generally use PVC drains and CPVC or PEX supply lines. These hold up well when installed properly. They do not forgive a bad joint, and a slow fitting leak can run for months behind a wall. That is why a surprise bill increase deserves a meter check, as described on our leak detection page.

If a pipe or tank fails tonight

  • Close the main water valve at the house or the meter and open a hose bib to drain the lines.
  • Switch off power to the water heater, and to any room with standing water if the panel is dry and reachable.
  • Clear small items from the floor and lift rugs before the water spreads.
  • Photograph the damage and the water heater label for your insurer.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell dispatch what you have already shut off.

Water, permits and HOAs

Your water provider depends on your address, so check the utility name on your bill before you ask about water quality. We do not quote a hardness number we cannot source, and you can test your tap yourself or ask your utility for its latest quality report.
