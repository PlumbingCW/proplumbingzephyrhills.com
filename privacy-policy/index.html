Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Call (813) 471-5608

This policy explains what information Zephyrhills Pro Plumbing collects and how we use it. It applies to this website and to calls to our dispatch line.

Information we collect

What we receive

  • Information you give us on a call, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem.
  • Call details, including the time, the number you called from and, if recorded, the audio.
  • Basic website data, such as pages viewed, browser type and approximate location, from server logs and standard analytics tools.
  • Emails you send to us.

How we use it

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, give you a price, complete the work and keep records of the job. We may use recordings to train staff and check the quality of calls. We use website data to keep the site working and understand which pages help visitors.

Call recording

Calls to (813) 471-5608 may be recorded and monitored for quality and training. If you do not want to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call or end the call.

Sharing

We share information with the plumber assigned to your job and with service providers that help us run calls and the website. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies and similar tools for basic analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Retention and your choices

We keep job and call records as long as we need them for the work, for legal reasons or for disputes. You can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@proplumbingzephyrhills.com. We will respond as the law requires.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new date on this page. Questions go to contact@proplumbingzephyrhills.com or by mail to 6305 Gall Boulevard, Zephyrhills, FL 33542. This policy was last updated November 13, 2026.

Plumbing problem right now?

The line is open 24/7 for emergencies. Tell the dispatcher what you see and what you have already shut off.
