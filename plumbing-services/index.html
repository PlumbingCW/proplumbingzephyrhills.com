How to choose between repair, replacement and prevention

A repair fixes the part that failed. A replacement swaps the whole fixture or appliance. Prevention, such as flushing a water heater, cleaning a line before it clogs or testing a pressure regulator, costs the least and avoids most emergencies. The right choice depends on the age of the equipment, how often it has failed and what a failure would cost you.

As a rule of thumb, repair a young item with one failed part and replace an old item that has failed more than once. Ask the plumber to explain the trade-off in plain words. You should hear the price of each option before work starts.

What to have ready when you call

Details that speed up dispatch

  • Your address and ZIP code.
  • What is happening and when it started.
  • What you have already shut off.
  • The age and type of the equipment, such as a gas or electric water heater.
  • Whether the property is a house, a manufactured home or a business.

Every service below is done to the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). None of this is required to get a plumber on the way. It helps the dispatcher choose the right parts and the right crew, which saves a second trip.
