The water service line runs underground from the utility meter to your house. When it leaks, the loss is out of sight until the ground turns soft, a patch of yard stays wet in dry weather or pressure drops at every fixture. Water line repair in Zephyrhills starts with confirming that the leak is on your side of the meter.

Usually the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the house, and the water utility owns the meter and the line before it. Check with your provider, which may be the City of Zephyrhills or Pasco County Utilities depending on your address. If water is flowing out of the ground, close the valve at the meter and call (813) 471-5608. The line is open 24/7.

Signs of a service line leak

What to look for

  • A wet or sunken patch in the yard that does not dry, even in a dry week.
  • A meter that keeps running after you shut off the house valve.
  • Low water pressure at every fixture at once.
  • A water bill that rose without any change in use.
  • The sound of running water near the meter box or the front of the house.

Our leak detection crew can pinpoint the break before we dig. If the leak is inside the house or slab instead, the symptom list on that page will help you tell.

Low water pressure: a service line cause

Low pressure at every tap is a classic sign of a failing service line, but it can also come from a closed valve, a failed pressure regulator or mineral buildup in old galvanized pipe. We check the pressure at the hose bib, the regulator and the meter side to see which it is.

A pressure gauge on an outdoor spigot is a cheap first test that tells us what to expect.

Emergency steps for a ruptured line

Before we arrive

  • Close the valve on the house side of the meter, or the valve inside the meter box if you have a key.
  • If you cannot stop the flow, call your water utility's emergency line and tell it the leak is at or near the meter, then call us.
  • Keep children and pets away from soft or undermined ground.
  • Photograph the wet area before the repair.
  • Note whether the leak is before or after the meter, so the right party fixes it.

What lives under a Zephyrhills yard

Pasco County Utilities' regional system reports that it draws groundwater from deep wells in the Floridan Aquifer and buys wholesale water from Tampa Bay Water. Zephyrhills addresses can be served by the city or the county depending on location, so your bill names your provider.

The line from your meter to the house is private property, and the soil it sits in is sandy and moves with heavy rain. A sandy trench is easy to dig, but it also lets a leaking line wash out the bed under the pipe.

Older homes may have galvanized or copper service lines that have corroded from the inside. Newer homes often use polyethylene or PVC lines, which fail at fittings. Knowing the material helps us choose between a spot repair and a replacement.

Hose bibs and outdoor faucets

A leaking outdoor faucet is a small water line problem with a big effect on the bill. We replace worn hose bibs, add vacuum breakers where they are missing and check whether the line behind the spigot is the real leak.

What affects the price

Cost factors on a water line job

  • The length of the run and the depth of the pipe.
  • Surface restoration such as a driveway, walkway, landscaping or irrigation lines.
  • Spot repair, trenched replacement or trenchless replacement.
  • Pressure regulator or shutoff valve work at the house.
  • Permits and inspection, which vary by address, so ask the city or Pasco County.

You get a clear price before work starts. Leaks that run for weeks can affect your bill, and some utilities offer a leak adjustment, so ask your provider. We can write down what we found and when we repaired it for your records.

Code and standards

The IPC covers water service pipe and calls for a pressure-reducing valve where static pressure exceeds 80 psi. Your water utility also sets rules for the connection at the meter, so the private repair stops where the utility's responsibility starts.

FAQ

Questions about water line repair in Zephyrhills

Do you handle water line repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Zephyrhills?

Yes. Call (813) 471-5608 any time. Close the valve at the meter if you can and describe what you see.

Who is responsible for the line between the meter and the house?

Usually the homeowner. Confirm with your water provider, which may be the City of Zephyrhills or Pasco County Utilities depending on your address.

How do I know if the leak is before or after the meter?

Close the house valve and watch the meter. If it still moves, the leak is between the meter and the house valve. Tell dispatch what you saw.

Can you replace a water line without trenching the whole yard?

Often yes, with trenchless methods, depending on the line and the soil. We tell you which options your line allows.

My pressure dropped. Is the water line the problem?

It can be. A closed valve, a failing regulator and a service line leak all cause low pressure. We test each.
