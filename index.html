24/7 emergency plumber for Zephyrhills homes and businesses

An emergency plumber in Zephyrhills has to be reachable when the problem starts, and the problem rarely starts at nine in the morning. A supply line lets go at midnight. A main line backs up on a holiday.

A water heater tank gives out on a Sunday and floods the garage. Our dispatch line answers 24/7 so you can describe the problem to a person and get the next available plumber sent.

Zephyrhills Pro Plumbing works from Gall Boulevard and covers Zephyrhills and the nearby towns of eastern Pasco County. We handle the common emergencies that fill a plumber's day: burst pipes, clogged drains, sewer backups, failed water heaters, running toilets and hidden leaks.

We also book planned work, such as water heater installs, repiping and fixture upgrades, during the day. All of it follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and the manufacturer's instructions for the equipment.

What Zephyrhills houses ask of a plumber

Zephyrhills is a city of mixed ages. Homes near the Historic District downtown, listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2001, date from the early twentieth century, and older supply and drain pipe in houses like these can corrode from the inside. If you own one, a stained fixture or a drop in pressure is worth a call before it becomes a burst.

The city also has many manufactured-home communities and seasonal residents. A home that sits empty for the summer can wake up in the fall with a stuck shutoff valve or a heater full of sediment. The plumbing on those properties is different, with lines often under the floor in the belly, so tell dispatch the home type when you call.

What should I do before the emergency plumber arrives?

Close the main water valve if water is flowing, and stop using drains if sewage is backing up. Turn off the water heater power or gas if the tank is leaking. Keep people away from wet outlets and move valuables out of the water's path.
