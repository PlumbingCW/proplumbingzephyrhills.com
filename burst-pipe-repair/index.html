A burst pipe is the plumbing emergency people recognize at once. Water sprays or pours from a split line, the floor is wet within minutes and the damage spreads fast. In Zephyrhills the usual causes are corroded copper and galvanized pipe, failed fittings, old polybutylene supply lines and, less often, a hard freeze. The first job is to stop the water.

Close the main shutoff valve, then call (813) 471-5608. Our dispatch line is open 24/7 and we send the earliest available plumber. If you are not sure whether the pipe is the problem or a hidden leak is, our leak detection page covers the quieter cases.

The first ten minutes

If a pipe has burst, do this first

  • Close the main water shutoff, usually at the meter box or on the house at the street side.
  • Open a faucet at the lowest point and one upstairs to drain the lines.
  • Switch off the breakers for rooms with standing water if you can reach the panel safely, and leave it if the panel is wet.
  • Turn off the water heater to avoid damage if it drains with the lines.
  • Move valuables, then photograph the damage before you clean up.

Freeze damage is rare in this part of Florida, which is why it catches people off guard. Freeze watches and warnings reached Pasco County in January 2026, and exposed lines, hose bibs and pipes in unheated spaces are the ones at risk. Outdoor lines and irrigation backflow assemblies split first.

How we repair a burst pipe

  1. Confirm the water is off. We test the shutoff and check that the system is drained.
  2. Find the failed section. We follow the wet area to the split and check the rest of the run for weakness.
  3. Cut and replace. The failed section comes out, and we splice in new pipe with proper fittings.
  4. Pressure test. We restore water slowly and watch every joint under pressure.
  5. Advise on the rest. If the pipe is old, we tell you where else it may fail.

After the repair: drying and insurance

Wet drywall and flooring need to dry quickly, usually within a day or two, or mold starts. A restoration company handles drying. We stop the source and make the plumbing safe. Most homeowners policies cover sudden bursts and the resulting water damage, though not the old pipe itself. Photograph the damage, save receipts and call your insurer early.

Pipe materials in the local housing stock

Zephyrhills includes homes from many eras. Houses near the Historic District may have galvanized or copper supply lines that are decades old. Homes from the 1980s and early 1990s may have polybutylene. Newer homes use PEX or CPVC. Knowing the pipe material changes what we recommend after the break, because a house full of the same aging pipe tends to fail again.

Manufactured homes use their own supply systems, often with flexible lines under the floor. A snap in the belly is a different repair than a wall pipe, so tell us the home type when you call.

What affects the price

Cost factors on a burst pipe repair

  • The pipe material and how much of the run needs replacing.
  • Access: a wall, ceiling, slab or crawlspace takes more time.
  • Whether a pressure regulator or shutoff valve is added.
  • Drywall and flooring work, which a separate trade may handle.
  • After-hours dispatch.

You get a clear price before work starts. If it is a mainline break outside the house, see water line repair.

Code and standards

The IPC and the product standards it references govern pipe materials. PEX tubing is made to ASTM F876 and CPVC to ASTM D2846, and fittings must match the pipe they join. Mixing incompatible fittings is a common cause of a repeat failure.

Questions about burst pipe repair in Zephyrhills

Do you handle burst pipe repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Zephyrhills?

Yes. Our line is open 24/7. Close the main valve and call (813) 471-5608.

Where is my main water shutoff?

It is often at the meter box near the street or on the outside wall of the house. Some homes also have a valve inside near the water heater. Find it before you need it.

Will insurance pay for a burst pipe?

Often the water damage is covered and the pipe is not. Check your policy and call your insurer promptly.

Can a burst pipe be fixed the same day?

Often yes, depending on access and the parts needed. We tell you the plan when we assess the break.

Should I replace all my pipes after one burst?

Not always. If the pipe is old or shows corrosion in several places, replacement is worth pricing. We will show you what we see before recommending it.
